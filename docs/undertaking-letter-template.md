- Interactive AI voice & text mock interview preparation modules
- Career path visualizer and skill gap analysis tools

### 2. Unconditional Declaration of Non-Recruitment & Non-Outsourcing:
We explicitly confirm and undertake that:
- **No Recruitment Agency Services:** DecaJobs is **NOT** a recruitment agency, employment agency, headhunting firm, or placement consultancy. We do not act as an intermediary in hiring, nor do we participate in employment contract negotiations.

- **No Candidate Placement Charges:** We do not levy or collect any registration fees, interview fees, placement charges, visa fees, or employment commissions from candidates or employers.
- **Direct Employer Redirection:** All job listings aggregated on DecaJobs redirect users directly to official employer career portals and corporate Applicant Tracking Systems (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever, Workday) to apply independently.
